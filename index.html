Change any page in the box under it. The checker flags words that aren't in her sight-word list, don't use letters she knows, or have sounds she hasn't learned yet (like ar, ee or a silent e). The checker is a guide, so read each story once before you print it. To print: print single-sided on US Letter paper, cut each sheet along the dotted line, stack the top halves in order with the bottom halves underneath, and staple the left edge.
